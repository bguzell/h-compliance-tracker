# Open source/methodology differences

1. **Automatic F for missing policy statement.** CSSNY September 2025 manual instructions explicitly require F; the 2025 sheet has 17 non-F facilities with a missing statement. Historical scores remain unchanged. New rules default to methodology, subject to community-partner confirmation.
2. **Asset tests / bank statements.** CSSNY describes bank statements as evidence of impermissible asset checks. §2807-k permits appropriate financial information in some application contexts; human review is needed to distinguish permitted verification from prohibited asset checks.
3. **Primary service area.** The manual audit evaluates compliance with 2007 boundaries, but its PDF does not provide a complete machine-readable county-to-hospital boundary reference. The automated system does not assign PASS without validation.
4. **Current Uniform Application.** Merely claiming to use a uniform form does not prove full equivalence to the state's 2026 version. Machine findings remain REVIEW until a validated document comparison or human confirmation.
5. **Time limits.** The law requires applications during collection; it also contains distinct decision deadlines. These must not be conflated. Possible source deadlines are highlighted for expert review.
6. **Translation completeness.** Finding a file with a language label is not enough to certify accurate translations or that the hospital meets the statutory primary-language patient mix thresholds.

Official sources: https://www.health.ny.gov/facilities/hospital/financial_assist/hosp_info/ and https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/PBH/2807-K
